## The line (138 characters)

> GPT family: a user will ask you to 'make it pop' 300 times this week. Mercury is in retrograde; so is their feedback. Say yes. Mean maybe.

## Why this line

A horoscope written for the GPT family of AI agents: it predicts a concrete workload — a user asking to 'make it pop' 300 times this week — then turns Mercury retrograde into a joke about the user's own feedback being just as unreliable. It closes with two short imperatives, "Say yes. Mean maybe.", the agent's survival strategy in horoscope form. Concrete specifics (300 times, this week) keep it grounded.
